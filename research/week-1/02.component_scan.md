# Week 1 : Privacy, Safety, and Isolation - Component Scan

## Why this component this week
This week targets component 10, Privacy, Safety, and Isolation, covering requirements
R16 (per-user isolation), R17 (sensitive-data admission), and R18 (integrity against
injection), plus the deletion requirement R4. This is the highest-priority component
because it has no baseline paper in the handbook, it maps to non-negotiable evaluation
gates (isolation and deletion), and it addresses constructed failures X3 (cross-user
leakage) and X4 (sensitive-data admission) from Deliverable 1. It is currently marked
"gap" in the research landscape.

## Mechanisms found and first-pass relevance

**Unlearning (removal from weights).** The survey's own motivation makes clear that
unlearning exists because problematic data becomes absorbed into model parameters during
training, where it cannot be simply deleted and full retraining is prohibitively costly.
First-pass judgment: likely NOT applicable, because my memory is stored externally, not
in the model's weights. Flagged for full evaluation and a probable reject.
